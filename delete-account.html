Delete your FlashGenius account & data

FlashGenius (by NetMock) stores only your email address and subscription status. To delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Email netmockias@gmail.com from the Gmail you use in the app, with the subject "Delete my FlashGenius account".
  2. We verify the request came from your own email address.
  3. Within 7 days we delete your account record (email + subscription status) from our servers and confirm by reply. Any active ₹199/month auto-debit mandate is cancelled at the same time.

What is deleted: your email address, session data, and subscription record.
What is kept: payment transaction records already processed by Razorpay are retained by Razorpay as required by Indian financial regulations; we keep no other copy.

You can also request deletion of specific data (without closing the account) at the same email.
